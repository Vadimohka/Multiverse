# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Multiverse community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

Use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language or imagery, personal attacks, intimidation, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the repository owner's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Vadimohka). Reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially where possible. Project maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or ban contributions or participants that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
